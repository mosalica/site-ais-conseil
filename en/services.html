Our services

1. AI & Sensitive Data Assessment — our signature service

Before connecting an AI agent to your information, you need to know exactly what you are giving it. In 3 to 5 days, the assessment establishes:

  • An inventory of the data placed, or to be placed, at an AI agent's disposal.
  • Their classification into four levels: public, internal, personal, sensitive or regulated.
  • Role-based access: which role (public, employee, specific team) may see what.
  • The model choice: hosted locally or online, depending on your data's sensitivity.
  • Retention and logging rules, and the guardrails to put in place before any deployment.

What you get: a clear picture of your exposure, an applicable guardrail plan — whether or not you continue with us — and the one-page sovereignty sheet: where your data lives, who you depend on, who keeps the keys. It is the page you keep on your desk.

Tailored quote depending on the size of your business — credited toward your first deployment, once per client.

2. Secured Agent Deployment

We build custom agents for your repetitive tasks, for example:

  • Answering your clients' or employees' general questions from your authorized documents only, citing the source.
  • Sorting and qualifying incoming requests, and preparing drafts (replies, work orders) that a human approves before sending.
  • Escalating to a human any urgent request, any complaint, and anything involving personal data or a specific file.

Each agent has its own identity, only opens documents within its role, and refuses what falls outside its scope instead of inventing an answer. See Your Data & AI for our guardrails.

What you get: an agent installed at your place, with limited access, a log of everything it does, and your approval required for anything sensitive. Tailored quote after the assessment. The assessment amount is deducted, once per client.

3. Monthly Support & Agent Registry

To keep your assistants under control, month after month:

  • Monthly review of your assistants: which ones are active, what they are allowed to do, what they have done.
  • Access adjustments and a log review with you.
  • A registry kept up to date: your assistant list, checked regularly, together.
  • Workshops with your teams to spot tasks worth automating and services worth reinventing.
  • A measured 4-to-6-week pilot before any expansion: one task, one before/after metric, an honest report.

Tailored monthly plan.
