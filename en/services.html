Our services

Adopt AI without exposing your data: that is the thread running through our three services. They combine, or stand alone — always starting with the assessment.

1. AI & Sensitive Data Assessment — our signature service

Before connecting an AI agent to your information, you need to know exactly what you are giving it. The assessment establishes:

  • An inventory of the data placed, or to be placed, at an AI agent's disposal.
  • Their classification into four levels: public, internal, personal, sensitive or regulated.
  • Role-based access: which role (public, employee, specific team) may see what.
  • The model choice: hosted locally or online, depending on your data's sensitivity.
  • Retention and logging rules, and the guardrails to put in place before any deployment.

You receive a clear picture of your exposure and an applicable guardrail plan — whether or not you continue with us.

2. Adoption & Deployment of Secured AI Agents

We build custom agents for your repetitive tasks, for example:

  • Answering your clients' or employees' general questions from your authorized documents only, citing the source.
  • Sorting and qualifying incoming requests, and preparing drafts (replies, work orders) that a human approves before sending.
  • Escalating to a human any urgent request, any complaint, and anything involving personal data or a specific file.

Each agent has its own identity, only opens documents within its role, and refuses what falls outside its scope instead of inventing an answer. See Your Data & AI for our guardrails.

3. Service Innovation & Support

To go beyond a first agent:

  • Workshops with your teams to spot tasks worth automating and services worth reinventing.
  • Prompts and procedures tailored to your trade and your clientele, in English and in French.
  • A measured 4-to-6-week pilot: one task, one before/after metric, an honest report before any expansion.
Our founding principle: the agent never replaces the human. It serves the human: it lightens their work by automating certain tasks, and always leaves the human in charge of decisions.
