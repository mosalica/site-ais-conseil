About

AIS Conseil grew from a simple observation: businesses here want to adopt AI, but they are right to ask first what will happen to their data. Our answer comes from a trade — identity and access management — where that question has been the daily work all along.

The founder

Mohamed Salif Camara, an IT professional who settled in Dieppe, New Brunswick, with his family, is an AI consultant and senior cybersecurity expert with more than 12 years of experience in banking environments and critical infrastructure.

Specialized in identity and privileged access management (IAM/PAM), Unix/Linux security, and security operations (DevSecOps), he now puts that expertise to work for Greater Moncton businesses that want to adopt AI without exposing their data.

Passionate about emerging technologies — artificial intelligence, the Internet of Things (IoT), and automation — he builds AI agents designed from the start to protect the data entrusted to them.

  • Education: graduated in 2012 from SUPINFO (Master's in computer science), specializing in information systems and networks.
  • Automation and IAM/PAM: application systems automation; administration and team leadership on eTrust (IAM) and CyberArk (Vault, CPM, PSM, PSMP), in environments with more than 100,000 privileged accounts.
  • eTrust / SailPoint: access certifications, connectors and roles, and the full migration of accounts from the legacy access system to the SailPoint platform.
  • Access governance: metrics for executive committees, internal audits, and security patching automation.
  • Certifications and training: CyberArk Trustee, ISO/IEC 27001, data protection (GDPR), Venafi (PKI and SSL/TLS certificates), and certified artificial intelligence training at aivancity Paris.

That experience shapes our agents: their own identity, least-privilege role access, logging, out-of-scope refusal, and human validation — the same reflexes as for a privileged account, applied to an AI agent.

Rooted in Greater Moncton

We work in English and French with businesses and organizations in Dieppe, Moncton, and the surrounding area. Our first developments were carried out in exploration with the francophone school community of southern New Brunswick, on fictitious documents and public information — caution first, results after.

Our founding principle: the agent never replaces the human. It serves the human: it lightens their work by automating certain tasks, and always leaves the human in charge of decisions.
