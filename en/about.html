About

AIS Conseil grew from a simple observation: businesses here want to adopt AI, but they are right to ask first what will happen to their data. Our answer comes from a trade — identity and access management — where that question has been the daily work all along.

The founder

Mohamed Salif Camara, an IT professional who settled in Dieppe with his family, is a cybersecurity specialist with recognized expertise in identity and privileged access management (IAM/PAM):

  • Administration and team leadership on CyberArk (Vault, CPM, PSM, PSMP) for 3 years, in environments with more than 100,000 privileged accounts.
  • SailPoint for 1.5 years: access certifications, connectors, roles, and the full migration of accounts from a legacy access system to the platform.
  • Access governance: metrics for executive committees, internal audits, security patching automation.
  • Certifications and training: CyberArk Trustee, ISO/IEC 27001, data protection (GDPR), artificial intelligence training at aivancity Paris.

That experience shapes our agents: their own identity, least-privilege role access, logging, out-of-scope refusal, and human validation — the same reflexes as for a privileged account, applied to an AI agent.

Rooted in Greater Moncton

We work in English and French with businesses and organizations in Dieppe, Moncton, and the surrounding area. Our first developments were carried out in exploration with the francophone school community of southern New Brunswick, on fictitious documents and public information — caution first, results after.

Our founding principle: the agent never replaces the human. It serves the human: it lightens their work by automating certain tasks, and always leaves the human in charge of decisions.
